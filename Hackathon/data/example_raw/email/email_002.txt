Hi Sisko,

That sounds like the exact behaviour we have seen after applying the recent firmware patches to the edge routers.

The confusing part is that general connectivity looks fine at first glance:

- device reachability = OK
- BGP sessions = established
- CPU and memory load = normal

...but high-priority voice and video streams experience intermittent packet loss.

Before rolling back the firmware version or scheduling a full maintenance window, check the hardware queue drops and buffer utilization metrics.

If the interface counters show normal packet counts but output drops are climbing steadily, try clearing the interface buffer or toggling the adaptive queue management profile.

This has solved the issue in several cases I've seen across the regional distribution nodes.

I don't think this nuance is covered in the standard release notes yet. It is one of those things we have learned from troubleshooting in the field.
